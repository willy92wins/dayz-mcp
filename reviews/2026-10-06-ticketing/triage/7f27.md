## ROOT CAUSE
`MCPResult` is one flat Enforce class shared by every verb, so the serializer emits unassigned scalars as their defaults (`0`, `""`, `0.0`); `result_prune.prune_unfilled_fields` only removes scalars listed in `OWNED_SCALAR_FIELDS` / `NEVER_FILLED_SCALAR_FIELDS`, and the ~21 scalars the ticket lists have no entries, so their defaults reach every response.

## PROPOSED FIX
**Recommended (smallest, follows the established #183 pattern):** extend `OWNED_SCALAR_FIELDS` in `tools/dayz_mcp/result_prune.py` with owner sets derived by the same static census used by `tests/test_owned_scalar_census.py` over `MCPBridge.c` / `MCPClientBridge.c`. From the excerpts alone, two entries are already establishable: `"clicked"/"handler"/"user_id": frozenset({"ui_click"})` and `"count_total": frozenset({"entities_query"})`; the census supplies the rest. Behaviour before: `vehicle_telemetry` reply contains `clicked=0, handler="", user_id=0, …`; after: those keys are absent, and `ui_click` still keeps `clicked`/`handler`/`user_id` even at `0`/`""`.
