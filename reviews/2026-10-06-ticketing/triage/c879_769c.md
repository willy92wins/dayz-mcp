## ROOT CAUSE
DayZ player movement is client-authoritative: a server-side teleport moves the server replica but the owner client's local player keeps its current command state with no floor contact, so `PhysicsIsFalling` stays false and no linked/floor entity engages until the `CommandHandler` sees movement input — the character hovers until a `player_move` (the ticket's own control proves a 1 s move re-engages simulation and the engine link).

## PROPOSED FIX
Recommended (S, two small edits):
1. `tools/dayz_mcp/server.py`, `player_teleport`: add `settle: bool = True`. After a successful server bridge call, if the client peer is probeable, issue one client `player_move` with `phase="hold"`, `hold_s≈0.3`, zero direction (the existing client verb, `bridge_readiness.py:311`). Fail open: if the client peer is absent, not in game, or answers `player_move_busy`/any error, return the teleport result with `warnings=["settle_unverified", …]` instead of failing — mirroring the occupant-precheck fail-open at `CHANGELOG.md:124`. Before: caller sees `ok` and the player hovers until it moves; after: caller sees `ok` (plus `settle` echo or `settle_unverified`) and the client player resumes gravity and floor linking.
2. Same function's description (`server.py:4748-4765`): document that a teleport alone leaves the client player unsimulated until movement input, and that `settle=true` (default) sends a zero-length hold move to wake it.

Option B (documentation only): add the hover note and a "call `player_move(walk, hold_s=1)` after teleporting" recipe to the description. Smaller, but leaves every caller to remember the workaround.
